## Problem

Let `n` be a positive integer such that `n + 1` is divisible by `24`. Prove that the sum of all
divisors of `n` is divisible by `24`.

## Formal statement

```lean
theorem putnam_1969_b1
    (n : ℕ) (hnpos : n > 0) (hn : 24 ∣ n + 1) :
    24 ∣ ∑ d ∈ divisors n, d
```

## Proof

Since `24 ∣ n + 1`, `n ≡ -1 (mod 24)`, and `Coprime n (n+1)` gives `Coprime n 24`; hence every
divisor `d ∣ n` is coprime to `24`, i.e. a unit of `ZMod 24`. Every unit of `ZMod 24` is
self-inverse (`∀ x, IsUnit x → x*x = 1`, a finite `decide`). Cast the divisor sum into `ZMod 24`
and apply `Finset.sum_involution` with the involution `d ↦ n/d`: from `d·(n/d) = n ≡ -1` and
`d·d ≡ 1` we get `n/d ≡ -d`, so the paired terms `d + n/d ≡ 0`; no fixed point exists because
`d = n/d` would force `1 ≡ -1 (mod 24)`, refuted by `decide`. The sum is therefore `0` in
`ZMod 24`, i.e. `24 ∣ σ(n)`.
